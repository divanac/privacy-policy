Common questions

How do I request a refund?
Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com using the Apple Account that made the purchase.
How do I restore a purchase on a new device?
Sign in with the same Apple Account, then use the “Restore Purchases” option inside the app. If it does not appear, email us.
How do I cancel a subscription?
Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; access continues until the end of the paid period.
Where is my data stored?
Unless an app explicitly says otherwise in its own description, your content stays on your device (and in your own iCloud backup, if you use one). We do not host user accounts.
How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes the data it stored on your device. To request deletion of anything we hold, email us and we will confirm once it is done.
The app crashes or a feature stopped working.
Try updating to the latest version first, then restarting the device. If it persists, email us with the details listed above and we will look into it.
Privacy policy

Information we collect

Our apps are offered free of charge unless stated otherwise on the App Store listing. When you use one, it may automatically collect a small amount of technical and usage information:

  • Device IP address
  • Operating system and device type
  • Screens viewed within the app, and the time and duration of each visit
  • Crash and diagnostic data to help us fix problems

Our apps do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone input. Where an individual app needs a specific permission to work — for example, notifications or the camera — iOS will ask you first, and you can withdraw that permission at any time in Settings.

How we use it

We use this information to operate the apps, monitor stability and performance, fix bugs, and improve features over time. We may occasionally send required service notices — for example, a material change to this policy.

Third parties

We may share aggregated, anonymised data with trusted service providers who help us run analytics and crash reporting on our behalf. These providers are bound to use the data only for those purposes. We do not sell your personal information.

We may also disclose information when legally required to do so — for example, to comply with a subpoena or legal process — or when we believe in good faith it is necessary to protect rights, safety, or to investigate fraud.

Your choices

You can stop all collection of information by any of our apps by uninstalling it using the standard process for your device. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To make a request, write to au.ocunecu@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as it is needed to provide the apps and for a reasonable period afterward. You can request earlier deletion of any data you have provided by contacting us.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it. In some regions, you must be at least 16 to consent to the processing of your personal information; a parent or guardian may consent on your behalf where local law allows.

Security

We use reasonable physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect the information we process. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the apps after a change indicates that you accept the updated policy.

Contact

For anything at all — support, privacy, or a data request — write to au.ocunecu@gmail.com.
