Privacy Policy

Effective · 2026-08-14

Word Streak is built by OcuNecu. It collects no data at all — no analytics, no tracking, no third-party services. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Summary
tl;dr
  • We collect nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting.
  • No third-party SDKs or services of any kind.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no servers of ours to send anything to.
  • Whatever you create in the app stays on your device.
  • Questions — au.ocunecu@gmail.com

We collect no data

Word Streak does not collect any information about you or your device. Not anonymously, not in aggregate, not for our own use. In particular, it does not collect:

  • Your IP address
  • Your operating system, device type, or any device identifier
  • Which screens you open, what you tap, or how long you use the app
  • Crash logs or diagnostic data
  • Your location, contacts, photos, calendar, or microphone input
  • Your name, email address, or any other personal detail

There is no analytics SDK, no tracking, no advertising, and no crash reporting in Word Streak. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours that receive your information, so there is nothing for us to store, sell, share, or lose.

No third parties

Word Streak does not use third-party services, SDKs, or analytics providers of any kind. Nobody else receives data from the app, because the app does not send data anywhere.

Apple may provide us with its own anonymised, aggregate App Store statistics — download counts and the like — if you have opted in to sharing them with developers in your device settings. That is data Apple collects under Apple's privacy policy, not something Word Streak collects, and it never identifies you.

What stays on your device

Anything you create in Word Streak — settings, progress, saved entries — is stored on your device only. If you use iCloud backup or device sync, a copy may live in your own iCloud account, which is under your control and Apple's privacy policy, not ours. We cannot see any of it.

Deleting the app removes the data it kept on your device. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing to request from us, and no data-deletion request to make.

Permissions

If Word Streak needs a system permission to do its job — notifications, for example — iOS asks you first, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings. Anything accessed under such a permission is used on your device, for that feature, and is never transmitted to us.

If you email us

Support is the one place where we do end up with your information: if you write to us, we have your email address and whatever you put in the message. We use it only to answer you, we do not add you to any mailing list, and you can ask us to delete the correspondence at any time.

Children

Word Streak is not directed to children under 13. Since we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children either.

Security

The strongest privacy protection is not holding data in the first place, which is the approach we have taken. For the support email we do receive, we apply reasonable safeguards to keep it protected.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. If Word Streak ever needed to collect something, we would say so here and in the app before it happened.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, write to us at au.ocunecu@gmail.com.
